Quarterback Evolution Over Time

This narrative visualization explores how the NFL quarterback role has transformed from the early run era to today’s pass-centric offenses. Three scenes use D3 to show average passing yards per game, completion percentage, and the top individual QBs each year. The dataset can be found at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/kendallgillies/nflstatistics/data?select=Career_Stats_Passing.csv.
